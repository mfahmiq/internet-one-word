# Candidates — The Internet in One Word

Judge brief (weight order): (1) single word describing the whole internet;
(2) earns a laugh by itself; (3) true about the internet as a whole, not one
app/site/mood; (4) surprises, not the first obvious word; (5) lands in one
beat — spelled correctly, instantly readable, short. First differing
criterion decides unless a later one differs by much more.

## The 16 candidates (different angles)

| # | Word | Angle |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | doomscroll | the core activity — endless scrolling |
| 2 | unhinged | the vibe / tone of the place |
| 3 | brainrot | what it does to your brain |
| 4 | touchgrass | the cure — what everyone online needs to hear |
| 5 | arguably | pun — the internet is one long argument |
| 6 | scrollhole | rabbit-hole pun — getting lost in the feed |
| 7 | unread | the notification / inbox pile |
| 8 | ragebait | the business model — outrage as content |
| 9 | incognito | what everyone does in private |
| 10 | cursed | the weird content |
| 11 | buffering | the eternal wait / the experience |
| 12 | oversharing | everyone posts everything |
| 13 | enshittification | the decay of platforms over time |
| 14 | clickbait | the headlines |
| 15 | hellsite | the affectionate insult for the place |
| 16 | procrastination | why you're really here |

## Tournament (pairwise, criteria in weight order)

### Round 1
- **M1 doomscroll vs unhinged** → **unhinged**. C2: "unhinged" is a punchline
  verdict ("the internet is unhinged"); "doomscroll" describes more than it
  makes you laugh.
- **M2 brainrot vs touchgrass** → **touchgrass**. C2: "touchgrass" is a joke
  with a target; "brainrot" is a diagnosis.
- **M3 arguably vs scrollhole** → **arguably**. C2: the pun lands harder —
  the word itself IS the joke (everything online is an argument).
- **M4 unread vs ragebait** → **unread**. C2: the 47,000-unread pile is a
  funnier image than a content-industry term.
- **M5 incognito vs cursed** → **incognito**. C2: the private-browsing joke
  lands instantly for everyone; "cursed" is narrower.
- **M6 buffering vs oversharing** → **buffering**. C2: the spinning wheel is
  a shared, funny frustration; "oversharing" is true but dry.
- **M7 enshittification vs clickbait** → **enshittification**. C2: the rude,
  vivid word is funnier than a descriptive label; C4 also favours it.
- **M8 hellsite vs procrastination** → **hellsite**. C2: the affectionate
  insult is punchy; "procrastination" is a dry truth.

### Quarter-finals
- **Q1 unhinged vs touchgrass** → **touchgrass**. C2: a command with an
  implied story beats a flat verdict.
- **Q2 arguably vs unread** → **arguably**. C2: the argument pun is funnier;
  C3 also favours it (discourse across the whole internet vs. the inbox).
- **Q3 incognito vs buffering** → **incognito**. C2: the private-browsing
  joke beats the spinning-wheel sigh.
- **Q4 enshittification vs hellsite** → **enshittification**. C2 + C3: a
  bolder, truer word about the whole internet's trajectory.

### Semi-finals
- **S1 touchgrass vs arguably** → **touchgrass**. C2: the laugh is immediate
  and visual; "arguably" needs a beat for the pun to click.
- **S2 incognito vs enshittification** → **enshittification**. C2 + C3: a
  funnier, truer word about the whole internet than one browser feature.

### Final
- **touchgrass vs enshittification** → **touchgrass**.
  - C1: both single tokens; no difference (neither is a phrase/sentence/list).
  - C2 (first difference): "touchgrass" is funnier by itself — a punchline
    everyone online has heard, aimed back at the whole internet.
    "enshittification" is a clever diagnosis that earns a smirk, not a laugh.
  - C3/C4 favour the runner-up (truer about platform decay, more surprising),
    but not by much more — and C5 swings back to "touchgrass" (10 chars,
    instantly readable; the runner-up is 16 chars and takes a beat to parse).
  - Verdict: the best word makes a reader laugh and then nod. "touchgrass"
    laughs first; "enshittification" nods first.

## Champion

**touchgrass** — 10 characters, one word, no explanation needed.
